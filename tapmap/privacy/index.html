Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 27 September 2026. TapMap is a daily geography game run by George Barker (“I”, “me”), who is responsible for your data. Questions or requests: barkerg57@gmail.com.

What I collect

Everyone

  • Game results: the date, each round's guess, distance and score, and your total, for daily games and challenges.
  • A device ID: a random code made on your device, so a game is only counted once. It contains no personal details.
  • Country and time zone: the country your connection comes from (worked out by our database provider's network from your IP address; I don't store the IP address itself) and the time zone your device reports.
  • On your device: your progress, stats and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. TapMap uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

If you create an account

  • Your username, display name and, if you add one, profile photo.
  • How you sign in: your Google account's email address and name if you use Google, or a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider) if you use a username.
  • Who you follow and who follows you, and the challenges you send and receive.
  • When you create an account, the games already played without one on that device are moved to it.

Who can see it

  • Public: your username, display name and profile photo (so friends can find you), and, if you have an account, your daily-game points and rank on the global leaderboards (today, this week and all time).
  • You and followers you accept: your round-by-round daily results, stats, streak and weekly league.
  • Anyone with a challenge link: the challenge and the names, scores and guesses of signed-in players who played it.
  • Games played without an account are never shown to other players; they're only counted in overall totals.

Why I use it

To run the game and the features you use (your stats, streak, friends and challenges), and to see overall numbers such as how many people play each day. The legal basis is providing the service you ask for and my legitimate interest in running and improving it. I don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Services involved

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Data may be stored outside the UK, with standard safeguards.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Esri (map imagery), Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (landmark photos), jsDelivr (code libraries) and Google Fonts: your browser loads files from these, so they receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

How long it's kept

Account data is kept until you ask for the account to be deleted. Game results without an account are kept for overall statistics; they're linked only to a random device ID. Data on your device stays until you clear your browser's storage.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email barkerg57@gmail.com from your account's email address or tell me your username; I'll do it within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Anyone can play without an account. Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
